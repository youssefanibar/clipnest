Online tools can be convenient, but it is worth thinking about privacy and permission before saving a post. A public URL does not automatically mean that the creator has granted permission to reuse the content.

## Check your rights

Prefer your own content, media licensed for reuse, or content for which you have explicit permission. Do not redistribute another person's work or use it commercially unless you have the necessary rights.

## Protect private information

Avoid pasting private, account-specific or sensitive URLs into unfamiliar services. Read the privacy policy and check whether the tool sends links to third-party processors.

## Respect platform restrictions

Do not try to bypass login requirements, privacy settings, technical access controls or platform rules. If a post is not accessible through permitted methods, respect that restriction.

## Keep devices safe

- Be cautious of download buttons that open unrelated sites.
- Avoid installing unknown extensions or executables just to save a file.
- Use current browser and device security updates.
