A link can fail for several reasons. The issue may be the copied URL, the original post's availability, account permissions or a change made by the platform.

## 1. The link is incomplete

Copy it again from the platform's share menu. Make sure the URL includes its beginning and has no extra spaces.

## 2. The post is private or removed

Private, deleted, age-restricted or region-limited content may not be accessible to an online tool. Do not attempt to bypass the restriction.

## 3. The link uses a short URL

Some platforms use short share links that redirect. A tool may not support every redirect format, even if the link opens in your browser.

## 4. The platform changed

Websites update their link formats and media delivery systems. A third-party tool may need maintenance or an updated integration.

## Try these steps

1. Open the link in your browser.
2. Copy it again from the original app.
3. Check that you selected the matching platform.
4. Try again later if the service is temporarily unavailable.
