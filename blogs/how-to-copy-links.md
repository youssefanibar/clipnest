Before using an online tool, you usually need the link to the exact post. The wording of menus can vary by app version, but the process is generally similar.

## Pinterest

Open the Pin, tap the share icon, and choose the option to copy its link. Return to the tool and paste the URL into the field.

## TikTok

Open the video, tap Share, then select Copy link. Some short links redirect to a longer address; that is normal for many sharing systems.

## Facebook

Open the public post or video, use its share menu, and choose Copy link. If the post is audience-restricted, other people may not be able to access it.

## Check before you continue

- Make sure the URL is complete.
- Check that it points to the post you intended.
- Do not share private or sensitive links with tools you do not trust.
