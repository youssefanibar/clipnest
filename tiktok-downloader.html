How to use the TikTok tool

  1. Open the public video in TikTok.
  2. Tap Share and copy its link.
  3. Paste the link above and check it.
  4. Only save or reuse content when you have permission and the platform's terms allow it.

Common link issues

Short links may redirect, videos can be private or removed, and availability may vary by region. This starter validates the URL but does not download the video.
