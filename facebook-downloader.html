Facebook Downloader.

Paste a public Facebook post or video link to check its format.

This tool currently checks the link format. Media extraction is not connected yet.

How to use the Facebook tool

  1. Open a public Facebook post or video.
  2. Use the menu or Share option to copy the link.
  3. Paste the URL above and check it.
  4. Respect privacy settings, copyright and the platform's terms.

Why a Facebook link may not work

Some posts require login, have audience restrictions, or have been removed. This starter does not bypass restrictions or retrieve video files.
